Excel Preview Power-Up

A Trello Power-Up that lets you preview .xlsx, .xls and .xlsm files directly on a card — no download needed.

Connector URL (for Trello registration)

https://oonufrienko-at-breeze.github.io/trello-excel-powerup/client.html

All files are processed locally in your browser using SheetJS. Nothing is sent to any server.
